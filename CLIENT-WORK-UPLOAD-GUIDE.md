# Client Work Upload Guide

1. Upload the finished video to YouTube and choose **Unlisted** visibility.
2. Copy the YouTube video link.
3. Open the website’s `/admin/` page.
4. Click **Open Portfolio Dashboard** and sign in with GitHub.
5. Open **Portfolio Projects** and click **Add item**.
6. Enter the project title, choose a category, upload a thumbnail, paste the video link, and keep **Show on Website** enabled.
7. Save the changes.

GitHub Pages republishes the website automatically. Updates usually appear after the deployment finishes in the repository’s **Actions** tab.

YouTube is not mandatory. Vimeo links and direct MP4/WebM links are also supported.


## Website Development category

When uploading a website project in Pages CMS, choose **Website Development** from the Category dropdown.
